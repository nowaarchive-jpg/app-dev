## Why I love it

*Interstellar* blends science fiction with an emotional story about family, time, and exploration.

### Highlights

- **Story:** A crew travels through a wormhole to search for a new home.
- **Themes:** Love, sacrifice, curiosity, and the future of humanity.
- **Soundtrack:** Hans Zimmer's score makes every moment feel epic.
